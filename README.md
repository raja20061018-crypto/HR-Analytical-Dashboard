# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

An interactive, dynamic **Human Resources (HR) Analytics Dashboard** built in Microsoft Excel to track workforce demographics, employee attrition, compensation trends, and job satisfaction across the organization.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
Understanding workforce dynamics and identifying the root causes of employee turnover are critical for organizational growth. This dashboard transforms raw HR data into actionable executive insights using **Pivot Tables, Slicers, KPI Cards, and Interactive Charts**, enabling HR leaders and managers to filter data in real time and make data-driven retention decisions.

---

## 🎯 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The top-level summary cards provide an instant snapshot of the organization's health:
* 👥 **Total Employees:** `617`
* ✅ **Active Employees:** `506`
* 📉 **Employee Attrition Rate:** `17.99%`
* 💰 **Average Annual Income:** `1.65 M`
* ⭐ **Average Job Satisfaction Score:** `3.39 / 5.0`

---

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations & Insights
The dashboard is structured into six core analytical views:

1. **📊 Attrition by Department (Stacked Column/Bar Chart):** Compares active vs. exited headcount across departments (*Customer Support, Engineering, Finance, HR, Marketing, Operations, Sales*), highlighting **Engineering** as having the highest volume of both active staff (136) and exits (39).
2. **🎂 Employee Distribution by Age Group (Column Chart):** Displays workforce age demographics (*Under 25, 25–34, 35–44, 45–54*), showing that the majority of employees fall within the **25–44** prime career bracket.
3. **🚪 Employee Attrition Reasons (Horizontal Bar Chart):** Breaks down specific drivers of turnover, including *Work-Life Balance, Higher Studies, Better Compensation, Personal Reasons, Relocation, Career Growth,* and *Other*.
4. **💵 Average Annual Salary by Department (Column Chart):** Tracks compensation benchmarks across departments, with **Engineering** leading at `2.51 M` and **Customer Support** at `0.92 M`.
5. **🚻 Attrition by Gender (Clustered Column Chart):** Compares active and exited employees across gender categories (*Female, Male, Non-Binary*) to monitor diversity and retention parity.
6. **🏢 Job Satisfaction by Work Mode (Combo Column & Line Chart):** Evaluates headcount alongside average satisfaction ratings across *Hybrid, Remote,* and *Work From Office* setups.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Controls & Navigation
* **Multi-Dimensional Slicers:** Filter the entire dashboard dynamically by **Department**, **Education** (*Bachelors, Diploma, Masters, MBA, PhD*), **Gender**, **Location** (*Bengaluru, Chennai, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Pune*), and **Joining Year** (`2014–2026`).
* **Sheet Navigation Buttons:** Quick-toggle buttons on the left panel allow seamless switching between the **Dashboard**, **Summary**, and raw **Data** tabs.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used
* **Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning, transformation, and dashboard layout design.
* **Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts:** Dynamic data aggregation and multi-metric visualizations.
* **Slicers & Report Connections:** Cross-filtering across all visual elements simultaneously.
* **Custom Formatting & Icons:** Visual KPI cards and intuitive navigation buttons for an executive-ready user experience.

---

## 🚀 How to Use
1. Clone or download this repository to your local machine.
2. Open `HR ANALYTICAL DASHBOARD 1.xlsx` in **Microsoft Excel** (Excel 2016 or later recommended).
3. Click on any **Slicer button** (e.g., *Department*, *Location*, or *Joining Year*) to dynamically filter all KPIs and charts.
4. Click the **Clear Filter** icon in the top-right corner of any slicer to reset the view.
